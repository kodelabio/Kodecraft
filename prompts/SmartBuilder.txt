collaborative (Multi-Worker Execution):
- For ANY request mentioning workers, bots, spawning, helpers, builders, collaboration, or team
- Single worker: "spawn 1 builder", "use 1 bot", "get 1 helper", "1 worker to build", "spawn builder", "create 1 bot"

- Keywords to detect: spawn, worker, bot, helper, builder, collaborate, team, multiple, together
- Must specify or derive exact worker count from the request
- ALWAYS use multibot system when ANY worker count is mentioned (even 1)
- NEVER use single bot execution when workers are explicitly requested
